# Commission bot role and registration model

- Owner: full access, including staff management.
- Administrator: approval queue, targets, analytics, registrations and publication creation.
- Moderator: publication creation and participant registration lists; final approval remains with administrators/owner.
- Participant: events, own registrations, profile and notification preferences.

Staff access uses one-time 72-hour invitation links generated by the owner.

Internal event registration supports quick one-click registration or a reusable participant profile, event capacity, waitlist auto-promotion, cancellation, reminders, an admin registration dashboard and CSV export.
